Binge Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page plans a series watch with addition and calendar counting: the nights the episodes take against a nightly window, the total runtime, the night-by-night schedule, and the finish date. It uses no named constants. The episodes, their minutes, the nightly window and the start date are yours to enter from your own watchlist. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your shows, your evenings or your calendar and does not know whether the runtimes are real, the window will hold every night or something will pull you away, and it gives no verdict that a show is worth the hours or a nightly window is wise. Every number here is your own entry.

The watch

Episodes in order, split by spaces or commas. An episode longer than the window takes a night of its own.

The nights truth

Nights: the episodes packed in order into the nightly window, with what the last night holds.

The total truth

Total: the runtimes added up, in minutes and in hours and minutes.

The schedule truth

Schedule: what each night holds, in episode order.

The finish truth

Finish: the start date plus the nights, one night a day.
