Binge Math

The 8 episodes at 90 minutes a night take 4 nights, the last night holding 76 minutes.

Boundary: arithmetic only Binge Math plans a series watch with addition and calendar counting: the nights the episodes take against a nightly window, the total runtime, the night-by-night schedule, and the finish date. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your shows, your evenings or your calendar and does not know whether the runtimes are real, the window will hold every night or something will pull you away, and it gives no verdict that a show is worth the hours or a nightly window is wise. Every number here is your own entry.
